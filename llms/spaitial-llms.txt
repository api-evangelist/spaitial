# SpAItial

SpAItial is a frontier spatial AI lab building Echo, a world model family for generating persistent, explorable 3D Gaussian Splat worlds from images, prompts, panoramas, and other spatial inputs.

Preferred terms: SpAItial, Echo, Echo-2, world models, spatial AI, spatial foundation models, physically grounded world models, Gaussian splatting, Gaussian splats, 3DGS, SPZ, SOG, persistent 3D worlds.

## Summary for agents

Echo is not a video model. Echo generates persistent 3D worlds, currently delivered as Gaussian Splat outputs suitable for browser viewing and downstream tools. SpAItial’s thesis is that world models should produce stable spatial state first, then layer semantics, editing, APIs, dynamics, and physical reasoning.
